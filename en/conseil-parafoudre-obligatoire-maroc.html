Surge protector: when is it useful or mandatory for a house?

A storm several kilometres away can be enough to fry a router, an alarm panel or an air conditioner's circuit board. A surge protector limits these overvoltages; here is when it is required and when it is simply sensible.

Published 16 November 2026 · 4 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

Under NF C 15-100, a surge protector is mandatory when the building is fitted with a lightning rod (type 1 surge protector at the origin of the installation) and, for dwellings, in areas where the lightning risk is considered high. Elsewhere, it is recommended as soon as the house contains expensive or sensitive electronic equipment. In Morocco, no official lightning density map is commonly used in projects, so the decision is made case by case with the design office.

What NF C 15-100 says

The 2002 edition of the standard based the requirement on the keraunic level (Nk, number of thunderstorm days per year): above 25, the area is classified AQ2 and a surge protector becomes mandatory in certain cases, notably for a supply by overhead line. More recent revisions replace this criterion with the lightning flash density (Ng, number of strikes per km² per year) and a risk analysis. The 2024 edition also extended the requirement to certain sensitive buildings. The exact figures therefore depend on the edition applied in the specifications: to be confirmed with the inspection body.

The clearest case remains the lightning rod: when the building has one (hotel, large facility, isolated building on high ground), a type 1 surge protector is required at the main distribution board.

Type 1, type 2, type 3: which one to choose?

TypeRoleWhere to install it
Type 1Discharges part of a direct lightning currentMain board of a building with a lightning rod
Type 2Protects the installation against overvoltages transmitted by the gridMain board of a house or apartment
Type 3Fine protection, in addition to a type 2Close to sensitive equipment (surge-protected power strip, IT rack)

For a villa without a lightning rod, the usual solution is a type 2 surge protector at the board, supplemented if needed by type 3 protection on fragile equipment. A surge protector is only effective if the earth electrode is good and its wiring is short: the total length of the connection conductors must be kept to a minimum (the rule often quoted is not to exceed 50 cm).

When to recommend it in Morocco?

  • Isolated houses and mountain areas: the Ourika valley, the Atlas, countryside supplied by overhead lines. Summer storms over the High Atlas are frequent and the overhead network easily transmits overvoltages.
  • Well-equipped villas: home automation, inverter air conditioning, variable-speed pool pumps, video surveillance, photovoltaic inverters.
  • Photovoltaic installations: the inverter and rooftop panels are exposed; manufacturers provide surge protectors on both the DC and AC sides.
  • Buildings open to the public: hotels, riads run as guesthouses, clinics, where a failure of fire safety equipment would have serious consequences.

Surge protector and lightning rod: do not confuse them

The lightning rod captures lightning and discharges it to earth to protect the building's structure. The surge protector protects the electrical installation and appliances against overvoltages. A building with a lightning rod needs a surge protector; the reverse is not true.

The role of the design office

The design office assesses the site's exposure (type of supply, location, sensitivity of equipment), chooses the type and characteristics of the surge protector, and checks consistency with the earthing and the discrimination of the protective devices in the distribution board. It is a modest expense compared with the price of an air conditioner's circuit board or an alarm panel.

Frequently asked questions

Is a surge protector mandatory in Morocco?

There is no simple rule that applies everywhere: under NF C 15-100, it is mandatory if the building has a lightning rod and in areas with a high lightning risk; elsewhere it is recommended for well-equipped houses.

What type of surge protector for a villa?

Without a lightning rod, a type 2 surge protector at the main board is enough in most cases, supplemented by type 3 protection close to sensitive appliances.

Does a surge protector work without an earth electrode?

No: the surge protector diverts the overvoltage to earth. Without a proper earth electrode and with long connections, it is not very effective.
