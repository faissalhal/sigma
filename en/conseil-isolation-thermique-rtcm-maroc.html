Thermal insulation of a house in Morocco: what the RTCM requires

Morocco's thermal building code explained simply: climate zones, which walls and roofs to insulate first, and what it changes for comfort and your air conditioning bill.

Published 16 November 2026 · 6 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

A well-insulated house stays cool longer in summer and uses much less air conditioning. In Morocco, these performance levels are governed by the thermal building code (RTCM), approved by decree no. 2-13-874 of 15 October 2014, which sets minimum requirements for new buildings.

Six climate zones

The RTCM divides Morocco into six climate zones. The requirements depend on the zone: the hotter or colder the climate, the better the building envelope must perform. Marrakech is classified in zone 5, one of the most demanding zones for summer comfort.

What the code targets

  • The roof: it is the surface most exposed to the sun, and the first to insulate. Under an uninsulated roof terrace, the top floor becomes an oven.
  • External walls: cavity wall with insulation, or insulating blocks, depending on the target performance level.
  • Ground slabs: in zones 5 and 6, the RTCM requires ground slabs of air-conditioned or heated spaces to be insulated.
  • Glazing: its area, orientation and solar protection (overhangs, sun breakers, shutters).

The code offers two ways to comply: meet minimum insulation values element by element, or demonstrate by calculation that the building does not exceed maximum heating and cooling needs.

Who it applies to

The RTCM covers new residential and tertiary buildings (hotels, offices, schools, hospitals), excluding individual rural housing. Even when it is not mandatory, applying it to a villa remains one of the best investments in comfort.

The right choices for a villa in Marrakech

  1. Insulate the roof terrace first, together with the waterproofing.
  2. Shade south- and west-facing glazing from the summer sun.
  3. Insulate the most exposed walls and deal with thermal bridges (columns, slab edges).
  4. Then size the air conditioning for the insulated house: the required capacity drops significantly.

The engineering firm's role

The BET advises the architect on the choice of materials and thicknesses to achieve the RTCM performance levels, checks compliance by calculation when necessary, then sizes the air conditioning accordingly.

Frequently asked questions

Which thermal zone is Marrakech in?

Marrakech is classified in climate zone 5 of Morocco's thermal building code (RTCM), one of the most demanding for summer comfort.

Does the RTCM apply to my villa?

It covers new residential and tertiary buildings, excluding individual rural housing; even when it is not mandatory, applying it clearly improves comfort.

Which part of the house should be insulated first?

The roof terrace, the surface most exposed to the sun, then the glazing and the most exposed walls.
