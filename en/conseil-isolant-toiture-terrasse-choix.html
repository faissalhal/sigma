Which insulation for a flat roof terrace in Morocco? A comparison

The roof terrace is the part of the envelope that receives the most sun. Choosing the right insulation, and placing it correctly relative to the waterproofing, transforms comfort on the top floor.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

For a roof terrace in Morocco, the two most common solutions are extruded polystyrene (XPS) laid on top of the waterproofing, known as an inverted roof, and polyurethane or rock wool boards under the waterproofing. The choice depends on how the terrace is used (accessible or not), the waterproofing system and the available thickness. A lightweight concrete screed laid to falls insulates little on its own.

Indicative values: check the product's technical data sheet. The thermal resistance of a layer is calculated as R = thickness (m) / λ. Thus 6 cm of XPS at 0.033 gives R ≈ 1.8 m².K/W.

What thickness in Marrakech?

The RTCM thermal regulation (decree 2-13-874) sets, for Marrakech (zone 5), a minimum roof performance that depends on the type of building and the glazing ratio. A technical guide on insulation in Morocco indicates, for residential buildings in zone 5, thicknesses of around 4 to 7 cm depending on the insulation material and the proportion of glazing. These are regulatory minimums: for the comfort of an air-conditioned top floor, a greater thickness is often cost-effective. The exact calculation is the BET's job.

Installation rules

  1. Choose insulation compatible with the waterproofing system (bitumen, membrane) and approved by the manufacturer.
  2. In an inverted roof, use XPS only and ballast it against wind.
  3. Treat the upstands and parapet to avoid a thermal bridge around the edge.
  4. Protect the insulation during the works: foot traffic and stored materials crush it.
  5. Specify a light-coloured finish or ballast to reflect radiation.

Insulating an existing terrace

On a house that is already built, an inverted roof is often the simplest solution: if the waterproofing is in good condition, the XPS is laid on top of it, followed by ballast. If the waterproofing needs redoing, take advantage of the works to place the insulation under the new waterproofing or as an inverted roof. In both cases, check the height available under door thresholds and the height of the upstands, as well as the slab's capacity to carry the additional weight.

The role of the engineering consultancy

The BET determines the thickness required to comply with the RTCM and achieve the desired comfort, checks the load on the slab (gravel, paving on pedestals) and coordinates with the waterproofing contractor. See roof terrace waterproofing and insulation and the RTCM.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best insulation for a roof terrace?

Extruded polystyrene (XPS) in an inverted roof is the most common because it resists water and protects the waterproofing; polyurethane offers higher performance for the same thickness under the waterproofing.

What roof insulation thickness is needed in Marrakech?

A technical guide indicates, for residential buildings in RTCM zone 5, thicknesses of around 4 to 7 cm depending on the insulation and glazing; more improves comfort, and the exact calculation is the BET's job.

What is an inverted roof?

A roof where the insulation (XPS) is laid on top of the waterproofing and then ballasted with gravel or paving slabs; the waterproofing is thus protected from the sun and thermal shocks.
