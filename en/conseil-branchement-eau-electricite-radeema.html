Water and electricity connection in Marrakech: from Radeema to SRM

Who to contact, which documents to prepare and which mistakes to avoid to obtain drinking water, sewer and electricity connections for a villa or a building.

Published 16 November 2026 · 5 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

In Marrakech, water, electricity and sewer connections are now requested from the Société régionale multiservices (SRM) Marrakech-Safi. Launched in November 2024 under Law 83-21, it took over the activities of Radeema in Marrakech, Radees in Safi and ONEE (distribution) in the region. The principle remains the same as before: an application supported by the building permit, a study by the distributor, a quote to pay, then the connection works and the installation of the meters.

Who is your distributor? The distributor depends on the city. The SRM reform is being rolled out gradually: several regions already had their regional company by the end of 2025. Elsewhere, you may still be dealing with ONEE or a concession holder. Check with the municipality or on a neighbour's bill before preparing the application.

The steps of a connection

  1. Submitting the application at the customer office, with the supporting documents.
  2. Technical study: the distributor checks the service (existing network in front of the plot, capacity, need for an extension or a transformer substation).
  3. Quote for the connection and, where applicable, for the contribution to network extensions.
  4. Payment and works: construction of the connection up to the meter or the facade cabinet.
  5. Subscription and commissioning, generally after completion of the works and according to the distributor's rules.

Documents to prepare

The exact list is set by the distributor and may change. It usually includes:

  • the applicant's identity document and proof of ownership (land title, purchase contract);
  • the building permit and the location plan or site plan;
  • for electricity, the requested power and the installation diagram; for a building or a subdivision, the location of the transformer substations;
  • for the permanent subscription, the occupancy permit or the certificate of compliance, depending on the case.

Villa, building or subdivision: what changes

ProjectPoints to watch
Detached villaElectrical power (single-phase or three-phase), diameter of the water connection, level of the sewer network
BuildingRising main, meter room, possible transformer substation, individual metering
Subdivision or housing groupVRD drawings approved by the distributor, MV/LV substations on the site plan, acceptance of the networks before connection

An old Radeema developer guide, for example, provided for response times of around 15 days for a subdivision and 7 days for a detached building; these times should be reconfirmed with SRM.

Mistakes that delay the connection

  • Underestimating the electrical power: a poorly prepared load assessment forces you to upgrade the connection later. See our article on the load assessment for a villa.
  • Planning the drainage without knowing the depth of the public sewer: the house outlet may end up lower than the sewer.
  • Forgetting the location of cabinets and meters on the facade, accessible from the public road.

The role of the engineering firm

The engineering firm prepares the load assessment, the electrical diagrams and the plumbing and drainage drawings that accompany the application, and it liaises with the distributor's technical departments to avoid back-and-forth.

Frequently asked questions

Does Radeema still exist in Marrakech?

No: since November 2024, the Société régionale multiservices Marrakech-Safi has taken over the water and electricity distribution and liquid sanitation previously provided by Radeema in Marrakech.

What documents are needed for a water or electricity connection?

Usually an identity document, proof of ownership, the building permit and a plan; ask the distributor for the exact list, as it may change.

Who pays for the transformer substation of a subdivision?

The conditions for the developer's contribution to substations and network extensions are set by the distributor and appear in its quote; they should be negotiated as early as the VRD studies.
