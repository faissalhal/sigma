Our approach

Four phases, one point of contact

  1. Assessment and feasibility

    Review of the architect's drawings, site visit, regulatory constraints and an initial cost estimate.

  2. Design

    Calculation reports, choice of systems and equipment, coordination between trades.

  3. Tender documents

    Shop drawings, technical specifications, bill of quantities and detailed cost estimate of the works.

  4. Site supervision and handover

    Site visits, compliance checks, testing and commissioning.
