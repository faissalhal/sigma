Fire extinguishers: how many to install, which type, what maintenance

The right number, the right type in the right place and an inspection every year: the simple rules for equipping a house, a shop or a hotel.

Published 16 November 2026 · 5 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

The most widely used benchmark is that of French workplace regulations: at least one 6-litre water spray extinguisher per 200 m² of floor area, with at least one unit per level. Guides add that nobody should have to travel more than about 15 m to reach one. In Morocco, these benchmarks are widely used in studies; the requirements specific to your building stem from the fire safety regulation (Decree 2-14-499) and from the analysis of particular risks.

Which extinguisher for which fire?

TypeFire classesTypical location
Water spray with additive (6 L)A (solids) and often B (liquids)Corridors, offices, bedrooms, shops
CO2 (2 kg or 5 kg)B, and electrical firesNear electrical switchboards, IT rooms
ABC powderA, B, C (gas)Boiler rooms, car parks, outdoors, gas rooms; avoid in living areas because the powder soils and impairs visibility
Class FCooking oils and fatsRestaurant kitchens, near deep fryers

How to install them

  • Visible and accessible, preferably near exits and staircases, marked by a sign.
  • Wall-mounted, with the handle at a height that makes them easy to lift off (around 1.20 m according to guides).
  • A suitable unit close to each particular risk: electrical switchboard, kitchen, generator, storage room.
  • Protected from direct sunlight and extreme heat, especially on roof terraces in Marrakech.

Avoid the classic mistakes: an extinguisher on the floor behind a door, a water unit as the only means near an electrical switchboard, a small car extinguisher for an entire restaurant kitchen, or units grouped in a locked room. An extinguisher must be grabbable within seconds by the first person who sees the fire.

Example

For a 3-level riad of 150 m² per level converted into a guesthouse, plan at least one water spray extinguisher per level, a CO2 unit near the electrical switchboard, a suitable unit in the kitchen and, if there is a generator or a gas tank, a powder unit nearby. The final number also depends on travel distances and the layout of the premises.

Maintenance

  1. Regular visual check by the operator: unit in place, seal intact, gauge in the green.
  2. Annual inspection by a qualified company, with a dated label on each unit.
  3. Servicing and refills at the intervals set by the manufacturer and the standard followed, and after each use, even partial.
  4. Safety logbook kept up to date for a building open to the public.

An extinguisher is only useful if someone knows how to use it: short staff training is the most cost-effective investment.

The role of the engineering firm

The engineering firm draws up the layout plan for extinguishers and other firefighting equipment (fire hose reels, dry risers) as part of the fire safety study, and checks that they are consistent with the building's actual risks.

Frequently asked questions

How many extinguishers are needed per square metre?

The usual benchmark is at least one 6-litre water spray extinguisher per 200 m² of floor area, with at least one unit per level and no more than about 15 m to travel.

Which extinguisher near an electrical switchboard?

A CO2 extinguisher, suitable for electrical fires and leaving no residue on equipment.

How often should an extinguisher be inspected?

An inspection by a qualified company every year, in addition to regular visual checks and servicing at the manufacturer's intervals.
