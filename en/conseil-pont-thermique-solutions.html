Thermal bridges: where they occur and how to treat them

Insulating the walls without treating the columns and slab edges means leaving concrete radiators in the building envelope. Where thermal bridges are and how to limit them.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

A thermal bridge is an area of the building envelope where the insulation is interrupted or weakened, and through which heat passes much more easily. In a Moroccan building with a concrete frame and brick infill, they are found mainly at columns, beams, slab edges and lintels. They are treated by ensuring the continuity of the insulation, ideally from the outside.

Where are they?

LocationWhy it is a thermal bridge
Columns and beams in the façadeConcrete conducts heat much better than brick or insulation
Slab edges and balconiesThe slab passes right through the wall
Window lintels and sillsSolid concrete above and below the openings
Roller shutter boxesPoorly insulated, often hollow casing
Junction between roof terrace and parapetThe terrace insulation stops before the wall
Junction between wall and ground floor slabGround-bearing slab not insulated at its edge

Their effects in Marrakech

  • In summer: concrete exposed to the sun stores heat and releases it indoors in the evening; the air conditioning runs longer.
  • In winter: nights are cool; the inner face of the columns cools down, which can cause condensation, black marks and mould in the corners.
  • On overall performance: a significant share of the heat losses of a well-insulated building can pass through these junctions.

The solutions

  1. External wall insulation (EWI): continuous insulation covers walls, columns and slab edges. It is the most effective solution, in new builds as well as in renovation.
  2. Insulating edge blocks at slab edges and insulating cladding of columns on the outside, before rendering.
  3. Thermal break elements at slab-façade or balcony junctions, to be incorporated from the design stage with the structural engineer.
  4. Insulation returns on the parapet and around the edge of the roof terrace.
  5. Insulated shutter boxes or external shutters without a box.

These measures are decided at the drawing stage: once the concrete is poured, only external insulation or partial corrections from the inside remain possible.

The link with the RTCM

The Moroccan thermal building regulation (decree 2-13-874) sets requirements for walls according to the climate zone; Marrakech is in zone 5. Achieving these performance levels means not letting the insulation be interrupted by the structure. See thermal insulation and the RTCM and insulating brick and double walls.

How to spot them in an existing building. A thermal camera, used early in the morning in winter or at the end of the day in summer, clearly shows the columns, beams and slab edges that stand out from the rest of the wall. Without a camera, the signs are black marks or mould in the upper corners of rooms, damp behind furniture placed against an exterior wall and horizontal cracks along the slabs. These observations guide the diagnosis before a solution is chosen.

Frequently asked questions

What is a thermal bridge?

An area of the building envelope where the insulation is interrupted, typically at concrete columns, beams, slab edges and lintels, through which heat passes more easily.

Do thermal bridges cause mould?

Yes: in winter, the inner face of the concrete cools down and water vapour can condense on it, especially in corners, which encourages mould.

What is the best solution against thermal bridges?

External wall insulation, which continuously covers walls, columns and slab edges; in new builds, insulating edge blocks and thermal breaks can also be specified.
