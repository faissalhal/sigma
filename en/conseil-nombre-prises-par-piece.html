Minimum number of sockets and light points per room (NF C 15-100)

The standard sets a minimum number of sockets and light points per room. These are floors, not targets: in a villa, you almost always plan for more.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

Under the NF C 15-100 rules (2002 edition as amended, still widely applied), you need at least 5 sockets in a living room of less than 28 m² (7 above that according to most guides), 3 sockets per bedroom and 6 sockets in a kitchen of more than 4 m², including 4 above the worktop. Each main room also has at least one lighting point, on the ceiling or as a switched wall light. These minimums do not include the sockets on dedicated circuits (oven, washing machine, dishwasher).

As for circuits, remember that a 1.5 mm² lighting circuit supplies a maximum of 8 light points, and a 2.5 mm² socket circuit a maximum of 12 outlets (see how many sockets per circuit).

What is really planned in a villa

  • Living room: 8 to 12 sockets, including sockets near the television, the sofa and charging points; floor sockets if the living room is large and open-plan.
  • Bedrooms: 2 sockets on each side of the bed (lamps, chargers), one near the desk, one for the air conditioner if it is not on a dedicated circuit.
  • Kitchen: in addition to the 4 worktop sockets, sockets for the refrigerator, hood, microwave and small appliances, plus the dedicated circuits.
  • Outdoors: watertight sockets on the terrace, in the garden and near the pool, on a dedicated circuit protected by a 30 mA residual current device.
  • Corridors and hallways: a socket for the vacuum cleaner, lighting controlled from several places (two-way switching or impulse relay).

Heights and locations

Sockets are installed at a sufficient height above the finished floor (in practice at least 5 cm for a 16 A socket), and worktop sockets are not placed above the sink or the hob. Remember to put switches at the entrance to each room, on the door-opening side.

Draw the plan before cutting chases

The most economical approach is to place sockets, switches and light points on the layout plan, including furniture, before work begins. The engineering firm draws up this layout plan, groups the points into circuits and checks consistency with the board. For the regulatory framework in Morocco, see NF C 15-100 and Moroccan standards.

Frequently asked questions

How many sockets in a bedroom?

NF C 15-100 requires at least 3 socket outlets per bedroom, whatever its size; in practice 5 or 6 are often planned.

How many sockets in a living room?

At least 5 sockets in a living room of less than 28 m², and 7 above that according to most NF C 15-100 guides.

How many sockets in a kitchen?

At least 6 sockets in a kitchen of more than 4 m², including 4 above the worktop, not counting the sockets on dedicated circuits.
