Home water tank and cistern: how to size and install it properly

Water cuts, low pressure in summer, a villa on the outskirts: many houses in Morocco have a water reserve. Properly sized and installed, it is useful; badly installed, it overloads the slab and degrades the water.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

The volume of a cistern is calculated from your actual consumption and the number of days of autonomy you want. A family that uses 20 m³ per month consumes about 0.7 m³ per day: 2 days of autonomy therefore require a reserve of about 1.5 m³. Beyond a few cubic metres, it becomes a structural question, because each cubic metre weighs one tonne.

Calculating the volume

  1. Note the consumption on your bills (in m³ per month) or estimate it with the utility.
  2. Divide by 30 to get the daily consumption.
  3. Multiply by the number of days of autonomy you want (often 1 to 3 days in town, more off-grid).
  4. Add irrigation and the pool only if they must be maintained during a supply cut.

If the reserve is too large, the water is kept for a long time and its quality deteriorates, especially in the summer heat.

Where to install it

  • On the terrace: gravity provides some pressure without a pump, but you must check that the slab can carry the load, place the tank above a column or beam and protect the waterproofing under the supports. A cistern exposed to the sun heats the water and encourages bacteria.
  • At ground level or in a plant room: safer for the structure, in the shade, and combined with a booster pump to distribute the water.
  • Buried (concrete tank): large volumes, cool water, but a structure that must be designed and made watertight with a lining compatible with drinking water.

Choosing the tank

  • Food-grade polyethylene (HDPE), opaque and UV-treated: the most common solution for small volumes.
  • Sealed lid and a vent protected by a mosquito screen: no insects, no dust.
  • Overflow and drain-down outlet connected to a drainage point.
  • Float valve at the inlet and an isolation valve at the outlet.

As for prices, listings surveyed in Morocco in 2026 show about 850 DH for a 1,000 L plastic cistern and several tens of thousands of dirhams for large volumes (15,000 to 20,000 L); this is indicative, excluding transport and installation.

Maintenance

A drinking water cistern should be emptied, cleaned and disinfected at least once a year, more often if the water has an unusual taste or colour. Also check the condition of the float, the lid and any pipes exposed to the sun.

The role of the engineering firm

The engineering firm sizes the reserve together with the internal network, checks the slab or designs the buried tank, and chooses the location that balances pressure, structure and ease of maintenance. Placing a 2,000 L cistern on a terrace without checking is a common mistake, especially in older buildings.

Frequently asked questions

What size cistern for a house?

Calculate your daily consumption (monthly bill divided by 30) and multiply by the days of autonomy you want: for 20 m³ per month and 2 days, about 1.5 m³.

Can a cistern be placed on a terrace?

Yes, if the slab has been checked: 1 m³ of water weighs one tonne. The cistern must sit directly over columns or beams, on supports that do not pierce the waterproofing.

How often should a water cistern be cleaned?

At least once a year, with draining, cleaning and disinfection, and more often if the water changes taste or colour.
