Complete electrical renovation of a house: steps and cost

Rewiring an entire house is a major job but decisive for safety. Here are the steps, what drives the price and how to compare quotes.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

A complete electrical renovation includes removing the old installation, creating an earth connection, a new consumer unit with 30 mA residual current protection, new wiring circuit by circuit and fitting the accessories. Its cost depends on the number of points (sockets, lights, dedicated circuits), the condition of the walls and the level of equipment; it can only be reliably established from a wiring diagram and detailed quotes.

The steps

  1. Assessment of the existing installation: what can be kept (conduits, cable routes) and what must be removed. See our article on how to check the electrics of an old house.
  2. Power assessment: air conditioning, kitchen, water heater, pool, vehicle charging. It determines the power to request from the distributor and the choice between single-phase and three-phase.
  3. Layout and diagram: position of sockets and light points room by room, list of circuits, consumer unit diagram.
  4. Earth connection and main equipotential bonding, then equipotential bonding in the bathrooms.
  5. Chasing, conduits and wiring: ideally coordinated with the plumbing and air conditioning, before plastering.
  6. Consumer unit: 30 mA RCDs upstream of all circuits, breakers matched to cable sizes, spare capacity for the future, labelling.
  7. Accessories, testing and acceptance: testing the RCDs, measuring the earth resistance, checking circuit by circuit.

What drives the price

ItemWhat makes it vary
Removal and chasingType of walls (brick, concrete, rammed earth), making good the plaster
Cables and conduitsNumber of circuits, lengths, cable sizes (kitchen, air conditioners)
Consumer unitNumber of rows, RCDs, surge protector, load shedder
AccessoriesNumber of points, chosen range, home automation
EarthingType of soil, access, rods or loop
Low-voltage systemsRJ45, fibre, video, intercom

For comparison, specialist French websites give ranges of around €50 to €200 per m² for a complete renovation, depending on the condition and the range; these figures cannot be transposed as they are to Morocco, where labour is cheaper but some of the equipment is imported. For a reliable budget in dirhams, get at least three quotes based on the same specification.

Comparing quotes

  • Insist on a quote itemised by cost item: number of sockets, light points, circuits, brand and range of equipment.
  • Check the stated cable sizes and breaker ratings, and the presence of 30 mA RCDs and earthing.
  • Be wary of lump sums without quantities: they make any comparison impossible.
  • Allow for making good the plaster and paintwork, often excluded from the electrical package.

The role of the engineering firm

The engineering firm draws up the power assessment, the plans and the consumer unit diagram, writes the specification used to compare quotes, then checks the execution. The technical references used are the Moroccan standards of the NM 06.1.100 series and, in practice, NF C 15-100. SIGMA ICC carries out these studies for houses and riads under renovation in Marrakech.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a complete electrical renovation cost in Morocco?

The price depends on the number of points, circuits and the condition of the walls; only a detailed quote based on a wiring diagram can establish it. Have the same specification priced by at least three companies.

When should you redo all the electrics?

When the installation has neither an earth connection nor a 30 mA RCD, when the cables are undersized or deteriorated, or when your needs (air conditioning, fitted kitchen, pool) exceed what it can handle.

In what order should the electrical renovation be done?

After the structural and waterproofing work, at the same time as the plumbing and air conditioning, and before plastering, flooring and painting.
