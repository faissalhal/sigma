Villa electrical installation price in Morocco: 2026 benchmarks

Electrical quotes for the same villa can vary threefold. The gap almost always comes from the scope: number of points, quality of materials, low-voltage systems, outdoor areas. Here is how to read the prices.

Published 16 November 2026 · 5 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

There is no official price. Moroccan price guides published in 2026 give wide ranges, of the order of 220 to 450 DH/m² for a standard installation and 500 to 900 DH/m² for a higher-end villa, and estimate electrics at about 8 to 12% of the construction budget. Other sources show much lower amounts, because they only count labour or minimal equipment. These figures are indicative: only a detailed quote based on drawings allows a comparison.

What an electrical package includes (or not)

Meter-to-board link, board and protective devicesDistance to the meter box, single-phase or three-phase, brand of protective devices, number of RCDs, surge protector

Light fittingsOften excluded from the package; recessed spotlights, LED strips and architectural lighting weigh heavily

Air conditioning, pool, gate, outdoor areasDedicated supplies, buried cables, enclosures
Low-voltage systemsRJ45 network, communication enclosure, video surveillance, intercom, alarm, home automation

Why two quotes cannot be compared

  • The number of points: a quote with 40 sockets and another with 80 do not describe the same house.
  • Lump-sum quantities: "complete wiring" without measured quantities allows no verification.
  • The equipment: unspecified brands and ranges leave the door open to substitutions.
  • Exclusions: light fittings, chasing and making good, connection to the meter, procedures with the distributor.
  • Compliance: a type A 30 mA RCD for the circuits that need it, a proper earth electrode and correct cable sizes have a cost that some quotes do not include.

Where not to cut costs

  • Earthing and RCDs: they are what protect people.
  • Cable sizes: an undersized cable heats up, and is very difficult to replace once embedded.
  • The board: leave a reserve of about 20% for future additions (see distribution board and GTL).
  • Spare conduits: EV charger, solar panels, gate, pool house. A conduit laid during structural works costs little; a trench after the finishes costs a lot.

How to get a reliable price

The method that works is to have a design office draw up an electrical file (diagrams, layout plans, power assessment, specifications), then ask contractors to price the same bill of quantities. Offers become comparable line by line, and site supervision checks that what is installed matches what is paid for. To place electrics within the overall budget, see the cost of building a villa in Marrakech and the cost of a design office.

Frequently asked questions

How much do a villa's electrics cost in Morocco?

According to 2026 Moroccan price guides, around 220 to 450 DH/m² for a standard installation and 500 to 900 DH/m² for a high-end villa. These are indicative benchmarks, to be confirmed by quote.

What share of the budget do electrics represent?

Guides estimate it at around 8 to 12% of a villa's construction cost, depending on the level of equipment (air conditioning, home automation, architectural lighting).

How do you compare electricians' quotes?

By having each contractor price the same bill of quantities drawn up from drawings and specifications, with quantities, brands and exclusions stated.
