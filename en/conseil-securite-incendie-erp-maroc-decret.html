Fire safety for public buildings in Morocco: what Decree 2-14-499 requires

Restaurant, clinic, school, hotel, shop: how the Moroccan fire safety regulation applies to your project and when you need to deal with it.

Published 16 November 2026 · 6 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

In Morocco, fire safety in buildings is governed by Decree No. 2-14-499 of 15 October 2014, which approves the general building regulation setting the safety rules against fire and panic risks, published in the Official Bulletin in November 2014. For a building open to the public (ERP), it shapes the very design of the building: number and width of exits, fire resistance, smoke control, alarm, firefighting equipment. It is not an end-of-works issue.

What the regulation contains

For each family of buildings, the regulation sets the safety principles, the classification, the construction provisions, smoke control, fit-out rules, technical installations and firefighting equipment. In particular, it distinguishes residential buildings, buildings open to the public and high-rise buildings (IGH).

FamilyWhat determines the requirements
ResidentialHeight, number of dwellings, access to the facades for the emergency services
ERPType of activity (hotel, shop, healthcare, education…) and permitted occupancy (public and staff)
IGHFloor of the top level more than 28 m high, or more than 50 m for a residential building, according to published guides

For ERP, the logic of classification by activity and occupancy is comparable to that of the French regulation, but the thresholds and obligations to apply are those of the Moroccan text: that is the one to consult, not an approximate transposition.

When and how is the file checked?

  • At the permit stage: the file for an ERP includes a fire safety section examined during the review, with the opinion of the relevant services, including Civil Protection.
  • Approval by a certified engineering firm: the press reported as early as 2020 that urban agencies require a certificate from a technical engineering firm certified in fire safety confirming that the drawings comply. A certification field dedicated to fire safety has been created for engineering firms.
  • At the end of the works: inspection of the installations (alarm, smoke control, emergency lighting, extinguishing equipment) before opening.
  • In operation: maintenance and periodic inspections, staff training, posted instructions.

The text applies in principle to new buildings; for an existing building converted into an ERP (riad turned guesthouse, villa turned clinic or nursery), the change of use is the opportunity to bring it into compliance.

The points that weigh most on a project

  1. The escape routes and emergency exits, which depend on the occupancy.
  2. Compartmentation and the fire resistance of the structure and walls.
  3. Smoke control of corridors, staircases and large rooms.
  4. The alarm system and, where applicable, automatic detection.
  5. Firefighting equipment: extinguishers, fire hose reels, dry risers, fire brigade access roads.

The role of the engineering firm

The engineering firm is involved from the sketch stage to classify the establishment, calculate the occupancy, size the escape routes and define the technical installations. It prepares the safety report and the drawings, coordinates with the certified firm and the inspection body, then follows the implementation through to the tests. Redesigning a staircase after the permit costs far more than planning it from the start.

Frequently asked questions

Which text governs fire safety in Morocco?

Decree No. 2-14-499 of 15 October 2014, which approves the general building regulation setting the safety rules against fire and panic risks in buildings.

From what height is a building a high-rise (IGH) in Morocco?

According to the guides published on the regulation, when the floor of the top level is more than 28 m high, or 50 m for a residential building.

Is a certified engineering firm needed for fire safety?

For the projects concerned, urban agencies have required since 2020 that the drawings be approved by a technical engineering firm certified in fire safety, according to the Moroccan press.
