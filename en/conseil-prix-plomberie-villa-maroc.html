Villa plumbing cost in Morocco: budget items and what to expect

The plumbing and sanitary package weighs less than the structural work, but it concentrates a lot of poor workmanship. Here is how its price breaks down and how to compare quotes.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

For a villa with a basement, ground floor and upper floor, an article in La Vie Éco estimated basic plumbing at about 60,000 DH, excluding high-end fixtures (an old figure, to be updated with a quote); sanitary ware comes on top (at least 13,000 DH for four entry-level bathrooms, with WCs at 700 to 2,000 DH each). These figures are indicative: the real budget depends above all on the number of water outlets, the hot water production and the level of finish.

What the plumbing package includes

ItemContent
Water supplyconnection after the meter, pressure reducer, manifolds, pipes to each fixture
DrainagePVC from the fixtures, stacks, vents, drains to the inspection chamber
Hot waterelectric, gas or solar water heater, safety devices, mixer taps
Sanitary fixturesWCs, washbasins, showers, bathtubs, sinks, taps
Equipmentbooster pump, cistern, water softener, lifting pump
Testing and acceptancepressure test, flow tests, flushing, as-built drawings

What makes the price vary

  • The number of water outlets: each bathroom adds supply, drainage and fixtures. This is the main cost factor.
  • The material: PPR, multilayer, PEX or copper differ in both price and installation time (see which pipe to choose).
  • Hot water: one electric water heater per bathroom, central production or a solar water heater change the budget significantly.
  • Additional equipment: booster pump, cistern, water softener, hammam, pool, irrigation.
  • The range of sanitary ware: from a few hundred dirhams for an entry-level WC to several thousand for a wall-hung WC with a concealed frame.
  • The configuration: a riad with long distances, thick walls and few recesses costs more to fit out than a well-prepared new villa.

Comparing quotes

  • Ask for a quote broken down by item, with the brands and diameters of the pipes and the number of water outlets.
  • Check whether or not the sanitary fixtures are included: this is the main source of difference between two quotes.
  • Insist on a pressure test before the chases are closed and on the handover of as-built drawings.
  • Be wary of a price per m² of house: it says nothing about the number of bathrooms.

Savings that cost dearly

Leaving out the stack ventilation, embedding untested fittings in the slab, forgetting the shower recesses or the drain slopes: these savings are paid for in smells, leaks and broken tiles. Redoing the plumbing in a finished villa costs far more than the extra cost of a proper installation. To place plumbing within the overall budget, see also the cost of building a villa in Marrakech.

The role of the engineering firm

The engineering firm prepares the drawings and specifications for the plumbing package, with a bill of quantities that makes it possible to compare contractors on an identical basis. It then checks the sensitive points on site (slopes, tests, recesses) and carries out the acceptance of the installation. This file is often what makes the difference between a low price and durable plumbing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does plumbing for a villa cost in Morocco?

As an indication, an article in La Vie Éco estimated basic plumbing for a villa on three levels at about 60,000 DH, excluding sanitary ware; the budget depends above all on the number of water outlets and the hot water system.

How much does the sanitary ware for a villa cost?

Allow at least 13,000 DH for four entry-level bathrooms according to the same source, with WCs at 700 to 2,000 DH; high-end ware can multiply this budget.

How do you compare two plumbing quotes?

Compare quotes broken down by item, with the same materials and the same number of water outlets, and check whether the sanitary fixtures and the tests are included.
