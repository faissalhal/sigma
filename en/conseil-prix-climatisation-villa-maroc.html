Cost of air conditioning a villa in Morocco: price ranges

The price of air conditioning a villa can vary fivefold depending on the system, the number of rooms and the quality of installation. Here are the orders of magnitude for the equipment and what a quote should include.

Published 16 November 2026 · 5 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

For a villa, the budget depends first on the system chosen. As an indication, a villa equipped with five 12,000 BTU inverter wall splits represents about 16,500 to 25,000 DH of equipment in mainstream brands, plus installation and accessories. A ducted system or a VRV costs considerably more, but brings discretion and comfort. The prices below come from offers published online in Morocco and must be confirmed by quotes.

What comes on top of the unit price

  • Installation: mounting, core drilling, vacuuming the circuit, additional refrigerant charge if the lines are long, commissioning.
  • Refrigerant lines in insulated copper, charged by the metre, and the trunking or wall chases.
  • Condensate drainage, and a lift pump if necessary.
  • For a ducted system: insulated ducts, plenums, supply and return grilles, false ceiling and access hatches.
  • Electricity: dedicated circuits from the distribution board, suitable protection devices, sometimes an increase in the contracted power or a switch to three-phase.
  • Supports for outdoor units and waterproofing repairs on the terrace.

The factors that make the bill vary

  • Capacity: a poorly insulated villa in the Marrakech summers requires more capacity, therefore more units or larger models. Insulating the roof often costs less than the additional capacity.
  • Brand and technology: inverter, energy class, warranty and after-sales service network.
  • The complexity of the work: renovation, riad, great height, long lines.
  • Running costs: a cheap but inefficient unit costs more in electricity over ten years than a more efficient model.

How to read a quote

  • Require the brand, exact model and capacity of each unit.
  • Check that installation, lines (with the length included), condensate drainage and commissioning are itemised.
  • Ask for the warranty on equipment and on installation, and the response time in case of breakdown.
  • Compare quotes drawn up on the same basis: ideally, a common set of specifications.

The role of the engineering firm

For a villa, a room-by-room thermal assessment avoids buying too much or too little (see our article on capacity in BTU). The engineering firm chooses the appropriate system (comparison split, ducted or VRV), designs the networks, integrates the power supply and drafts a specification that makes it possible to compare installers on a like-for-like basis. It can then check the installation work on site.

Frequently asked questions

How much does air conditioning a villa in Morocco cost?

For five 12,000 BTU inverter splits from a mainstream brand, allow about 16,500 to 25,000 DH of equipment, excluding installation and accessories; a ducted system or a VRV costs considerably more. These amounts are indicative.

How much does a ducted air conditioner cost in Morocco?

Online offers show about 7,900 DH for an 18,000 BTU inverter ducted unit and 16,900 DH for 36,000 BTU, excluding ducts, grilles, false ceiling and installation.

What should an air conditioning quote include?

The brand, model and capacity of each unit, itemised installation with the length of the lines, condensate drainage, electrical work, commissioning and warranties.
