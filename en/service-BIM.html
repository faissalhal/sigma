Building Information Modeling (BIM)

SolutionsStructureRoads and utilitiesEnergy and thermal engineeringPlumbing, HVAC and smoke controlElectrical systems (CFO / CFA)Fire safety coordinationBIM

As an engineering consultancy, we specialize in the design, construction and management of buildings using the BIM methodology. This approach allows us to create information-rich 3D digital models covering every aspect of the building, enabling us to optimize the design, construction and management of the project.

We offer a range of BIM services to help our clients deliver their construction projects more efficiently, economically and sustainably. We can create detailed BIM models of existing or planned buildings, coordinate the various stakeholders of a construction project using BIM models, simulate scenarios to optimize building safety and resilience, and manage construction projects with BIM models to optimize costs and timelines.

Our goal is to provide our clients with bespoke BIM solutions that meet their specific needs. We work closely with our clients throughout the design, construction and management process to ensure that the end result matches their expectations and the highest quality standards.

If you would like to learn more about our BIM services or have any questions, feel free to contact us. We will be happy to discuss your projects and offer bespoke BIM solutions to deliver them successfully.
