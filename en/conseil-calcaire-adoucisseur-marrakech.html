Limescale and water softeners in Marrakech: should you treat water?

White marks on taps, a water heater that heats less well, clogged shower heads: limescale is expensive. Before buying a water softener, measure the hardness of your water.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

A water softener is justified when the water exceeds about 25 to 30 °f (French degrees of hardness). Below that, regular maintenance is enough. Hardness varies depending on the source that supplies your neighbourhood or your borehole: the first step is therefore to measure it, with a test strip, a test kit or the analyses published by the utility (Radeema in Marrakech).

Understanding water hardness

Hardness measures the amount of dissolved calcium and magnesium. One French degree (1 °f) corresponds to 10 mg of calcium carbonate per litre. When water is heated, these minerals are deposited: this is scale.

Borehole water, common in villas on the outskirts of Marrakech, can be very different from mains water: have it analysed before choosing a treatment.

What limescale costs

  • Water heater: the layer of scale insulates the heating element, which heats for longer and eventually burns out; the tank loses working volume.
  • Solar water heater: direct-heating collectors scale up quickly; an indirect circuit is preferable (see solar water heater).
  • Taps and showers: cartridges that seize, clogged shower heads, marked glass screens.
  • Household appliances: washing machines and dishwashers use more products and wear out faster.

Installing a water softener properly

  • Position: after the meter, the filter and the pressure reducer, with a bypass for maintenance and a drain for regeneration cycles.
  • Unsoftened water tap: keep one untreated water outlet for drinking and cooking, and do not treat the irrigation water, which would waste salt.
  • Setting: do not go down to 0 °f; a residual hardness of about 8 to 15 °f is often recommended to avoid water that is aggressive towards the pipes.
  • Maintenance: salt topped up regularly, resin disinfected according to the instructions, annual check of the outlet hardness.

Prices and alternatives

In Morocco, small resin water softeners are offered from about 2,500 DH according to listings surveyed; a model suited to a villa, including installation, costs considerably more. Magnetic or electronic "anti-scale" devices do not remove limescale from the water and their effectiveness is disputed by consumer associations. For a small home, a polyphosphate anti-scale filter upstream of the water heater can be a compromise.

The role of the engineering firm

In a hotel, a guesthouse or a villa with a pool and a hammam, water treatment is sized together with the rest of the installation: peak flow, hot water volume, plant room, drainage. The engineering firm integrates these elements into the project to avoid improvised additions after handover.

Frequently asked questions

At what hardness level is a water softener needed?

A water softener is generally justified above 25 to 30 °f; below that, regular maintenance of appliances is usually enough.

How can I find out the water hardness in Marrakech?

Ask your utility (Radeema) for its analyses or measure it with a test strip or test kit; borehole water must be analysed separately.

Can softened water be drunk?

It is advisable to keep an unsoftened water tap for drinking and cooking, and to set the softener so that it keeps some residual hardness.
