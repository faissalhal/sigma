Swimming pool electrics: zones, 12 V SELV and filtration panel

A swimming pool combines water, the human body and electricity: the most dangerous combination. The rules are based on zones around the pool, as in a bathroom, but with greater distances.

Published 16 November 2026 · 5 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

Around a swimming pool, NF C 15-100 defines three zones: zone 0 (the inside of the pool), zone 1 (up to 2 m from the edge and 2.5 m high) and zone 2 (an additional 1.5 m strip, i.e. up to 3.5 m from the edge). In zones 0 and 1, only safety extra-low voltage (SELV) equipment, typically 12 V, is allowed, and its transformer must be located outside zones 0, 1 and 2. The filtration electrical panel is therefore placed in a plant room or more than 3.5 m from the edge.

What is allowed in each zone

ZoneExtentEquipment allowed (principle)
0Inside the pool, footbathUnderwater lights and equipment designed for immersion, in 12 V SELV
1Up to 2 m from the edge, 2.5 m highSELV equipment, safety source outside the zones; no 230 V socket
2From 2 m to 3.5 m from the edgeSELV, or equipment protected by a 30 mA RCD under the conditions of the standard; sockets strictly regulated
Outside the zonesBeyond 3.5 mFiltration panel, transformers, ordinary sockets protected by a 30 mA RCD

Detailed conditions (protection ratings, above-ground pools, buried plant rooms) depend on the edition of the standard applied: the section on swimming pools is covered by a specific part of NF C 15-100. To be confirmed by the inspection body for a building open to the public.

The electrical equipment of a swimming pool

  • Filtration panel: circuit breakers, 30 mA RCD, timer, pump contactor, transformer for the lights. It is installed in the plant room, sheltered from water and ventilated, with an RCD of a type suited to variable-speed drives if the pump is variable-speed.
  • Lights: supplied at 12 V by a safety transformer, with a cable sized for the length to the pool (voltage drop is significant at 12 V).
  • Heat pump, salt chlorinator, pH control: each on a protected circuit, outside the zones.
  • Pool deck lighting: bollards and wall lights placed outside zones 0 and 1, or in SELV.

Equipotential bonding and earthing

All the conductive parts of the pool and its surroundings are connected to each other and to earth: pool reinforcement, ladders, handrails, nozzles and metal parts, metal pipes, deck grilles. This equipotential bonding prevents a dangerous potential difference from arising between two parts that a swimmer can touch. The equipotential mesh is prepared at the reinforcement stage: it cannot be added once the concrete is poured. See also main equipotential bonding.

Mistakes seen on site

  • Filtration panel installed against the pool, in a pit that fills with water.
  • Second-hand "230 V" lights, or a 12 V transformer without safety isolation.
  • Extension lead run from the house for a robot cleaner or a pump.
  • Cables buried without conduit or warning mesh, later cut by the gardener.
  • Forgetting to bond the reinforcement when the concrete is poured.

The role of the design office

For a pool at a villa, riad or hotel, the design office marks the zones on the plan, positions the plant room, sizes the cables (including at 12 V) and defines the protective devices. For a building open to the public, these choices are checked by the inspection body. For the overall budget of a pool, see the price of a swimming pool in Morocco.

Frequently asked questions

How far from the pool can a socket be placed?

Ordinary 230 V sockets are placed outside the zones, i.e. more than 3.5 m from the edge of the pool, and are protected by a 30 mA RCD.

Why are pool lights 12 V?

In zones 0 and 1, only safety extra-low voltage (SELV), in practice 12 V, is allowed, with a transformer installed outside zones 0, 1 and 2.

Where should the filtration panel be installed?

In a dry, ventilated plant room, or more than 3.5 m from the edge of the pool, never in a pit exposed to water or against the coping.
