Air conditioning maintenance: how often and what to check?

A poorly maintained air conditioner uses more power, cools less and can spread odours or mould. A few simple steps and an annual visit are enough in most homes.

Published 16 November 2026 · 5 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

The simple rule: clean the indoor unit filters about once a month during the season of use, and have the installation serviced once a year by a refrigeration technician, ideally before summer. In Marrakech, where dust is abundant and units run for several months in a row, this frequency is no luxury.

The maintenance schedule

TaskIndicative frequencyWho
Cleaning the indoor unit filtersAbout every month in season, more often if dustyThe occupant
Clearing the outdoor unit (leaves, bags, sand)Before each seasonThe occupant
Checking condensate drainageAt the start of the seasonThe occupant or the technician
Full service: pressures, temperatures, leak tightness, electrical connections, coil cleaningOnce a yearRefrigeration technician
Thorough cleaning of the fan and condensate trayDepending on fouling, often annualTechnician

What you can do yourself

  • The filters: switch the unit off, open the front panel, remove the filters, wash them in lukewarm water with mild soap, let them dry in the shade before putting them back.
  • The outdoor unit: keep at least the clearance specified by the manufacturer around the unit, remove debris, never cover it while it is running.
  • The remote control: a setpoint of around 25 to 26 °C in summer avoids running the unit at full power for a few degrees too many.

What is left to the professional

Only a qualified technician should work on the refrigerant circuit. A drop in performance is often a sign of a refrigerant leak: recharging without looking for the leak means paying several times for the same problem. During the service, the technician measures pressures and temperatures, checks leak tightness, cleans the heat exchange coils, checks the condensate drainage and the electrical terminals.

In France, periodic leak checks are mandatory above certain refrigerant charge or capacity thresholds; in Morocco, in the absence of an equivalent obligation for small domestic units, an annual check remains good practice. For large installations (VRV, hotels, offices), a maintenance contract is strongly recommended.

Signs that maintenance is needed

  • The cooling is weaker than before or the unit runs without stopping.
  • A musty smell appears on start-up.
  • Water drips from the indoor unit: see our article on condensate drainage.
  • Frost forms on the pipes or the outdoor unit makes an unusual noise.
  • The electricity bill rises for no reason.

As for the budget, maintenance rates in Morocco vary widely depending on the city, the number of units and the scope of the work; online listings do not always detail what is included. Ask for a quote specifying the tasks carried out (cleaning, leak check, measurements, possible recharge) rather than a simple price "per split".

Good design for easy maintenance

Easy maintenance is planned from the design stage: accessible indoor units, access hatches under ducted units, outdoor units on an accessible terrace rather than on the façade. The engineering firm includes these accesses in the drawings and drafts the maintenance logbook handed over to the owner.

Frequently asked questions

How often should air conditioner filters be cleaned?

About once a month during the season of use, and more often in a dusty environment such as Marrakech.

Should air conditioning be serviced every year?

Yes, an annual service by a refrigeration technician, ideally before summer, makes it possible to check leak tightness, pressures, condensate drainage and electrical connections.

Why does my air conditioning smell musty?

Most often because of dirty filters, coil or condensate tray; cleaning the filters followed by a thorough clean by a technician usually solves the problem.
