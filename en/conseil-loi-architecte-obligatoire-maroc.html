Is an architect mandatory in Morocco? Laws 12-90 and 016-89

To build in an urban area in Morocco, an architect is not optional. What the law requires, the possible exceptions and how roles are shared with the engineering consultancy.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

Yes, in the vast majority of cases. Law 12-90 on urban planning requires the use of an architect in private practice for any new building and for any modification of an existing building requiring a building permit. The profession itself is organised by Law No. 016-89, which regulates the practice of the architectural profession and establishes the National Order of Architects.

What the architect does

  • Design: sketch, preliminary design, permit drawings compliant with the zoning plan (heights, setbacks, plot ratio).
  • Permit application submitted on the Rokhas platform.
  • Site supervision for architectural compliance.
  • Certificate of completion of works, required to obtain the occupancy permit.

Special cases

Lighter regimes exist, notably for certain buildings in rural areas, and some practical websites mention floor-area thresholds. These exceptions depend on the applicable texts and local regulations: do not assume them, check with the municipality or the urban agency before launching a project without an architect. For a permit in a city (Marrakech, Casablanca, Rabat, Agadir…), the application is not processed without an architect.

The two are complementary: the architect designs the building, the BET makes it buildable and safe. In practice, the permit application includes technical studies, particularly structural ones.

Liabilities. The architect and the engineer contracted directly by the client are subject to decennial liability under Article 769 of the DOC. Since mandatory construction insurance came into force in late 2024, they must also hold decennial civil liability insurance.

Choosing well

  1. Check registration with the National Order of Architects.
  2. Sign a contract specifying the tasks (design only or full supervision) and the fees.
  3. Involve the BET from the sketch stage: structure and networks influence the architecture.

Fees and the contract. The architect's fees depend on the tasks entrusted and the size of the project; they are set by contract. Ask whether site supervision is included, how many visits are planned, and who prepares the construction drawings. An architect who only prepares the permit application leaves you alone with the contractor: their signature at the end of the works, required for the occupancy permit, assumes they have been able to verify that the works comply. So compare offers on the basis of equivalent tasks, not just on price.

Frequently asked questions

Is an architect mandatory to build a house in Morocco?

Yes, in the vast majority of cases: Law 12-90 requires an architect in private practice for any construction or modification requiring a building permit; lighter regimes exist mainly in rural areas, to be checked locally.

Which law regulates the architectural profession in Morocco?

Law No. 016-89 on the practice of the architectural profession, which also establishes the National Order of Architects.

Does the architect replace the engineering consultancy?

No: the architect designs and obtains the permit; the BET calculates the structure and designs the technical networks.
