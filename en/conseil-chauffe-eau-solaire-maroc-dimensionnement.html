Solar water heater in Morocco: what capacity should you choose?

With Morocco's sunshine, a solar water heater is almost always a good investment. It still needs to be sized and installed correctly.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

A solar water heater is sized on the basis of about 50 litres of hot water per person per day. In practice, a 150-litre tank suits 2 or 3 people, a 200-litre tank with one collector a family of 4, and a 300-litre tank with two collectors 5 or 6 people. A villa with several bathrooms or a guesthouse requires a more precise calculation.

The sizing table

These benchmarks correspond to the ranges sold in Morocco; they assume ordinary use (showers, kitchen). A large bathtub, a high-flow shower or an indoor pool completely change the requirement.

Thermosiphon or forced circulation

  • Thermosiphon (compact unit): the tank sits above the collector, on the terrace. The heated water rises on its own, with no pump or electricity. This is the most widespread solution for individual houses.
  • Forced circulation: the tank is indoors (plant room), and a pump and controller circulate the fluid. More discreet, suited to riads where nothing should be visible on the terrace, and to large volumes.

Points not to overlook

  • Orientation and tilt: collectors facing due south, tilted at roughly the latitude of the site (about 30° in Marrakech), with no shading.
  • Weight: a full 300 L unit weighs several hundred kilos. The terrace slab and the fixings must be checked, as well as wind resistance.
  • Waterproofing: the support feet must not pierce the membrane without proper treatment (see flat roof waterproofing).
  • Limescale: in hard-water regions such as Marrakech, an indirect-circuit model (heat exchanger) scales up far less than one where the water is heated directly in the collector. See also limescale and water softeners.
  • Safety: relief valve or safety group, expansion vessel if needed, and a thermostatic mixing valve at the outlet, because the stored water can become scalding in summer.
  • Backup: an electric heating element for overcast days, preferably controlled by a timer so it only heats when necessary. Health authorities recommend producing hot water at 55 °C or more to limit the risk of legionella.

Price and payback

Prices vary widely depending on capacity, brand (imported or made in Morocco) and type of installation; most installers give them on a quotation basis. Compare at equal capacity, including installation, supports and the tank warranty. With such strong sunshine, a solar water heater replaces most electric or gas hot water production.

The role of the engineering firm

For a guesthouse, an apartment building or a high-end villa, the engineering firm calculates the actual needs, chooses between individual and collective installations, checks the load on the slab and integrates the solar system into the project's thermal calculation.

Frequently asked questions

What solar water heater capacity for 4 people?

A 200-litre tank with one collector generally suits 4 people, on the basis of about 50 litres of hot water per person per day.

Does a solar water heater need a backup heating element?

Yes, it is recommended for overcast winter days; controlled by a timer, it only runs when the sun is not enough.

Does a solar water heater scale up in Marrakech?

Yes, if the hard water circulates directly through the collector; an indirect-circuit model (heat exchanger) and periodic descaling greatly limit this problem.
