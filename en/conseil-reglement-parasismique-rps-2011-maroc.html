Seismic code RPS 2011 in Morocco: what it means for your house

Seismic zoning, design rules, ring beams and reinforcement: what the seismic code actually requires of a villa or a riad in Morocco.

Published 16 November 2026 · 7 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

The Al Haouz earthquake of 8 September 2023 was a reminder that the Marrakech region is exposed to seismic risk. In Morocco, the earthquake resistance of buildings is governed by the seismic building code, RPS 2000, whose revised 2011 version is today the reference for engineering firms.

Finer zoning

The 2011 version replaced the former division into three zones with a five-zone map (numbered 0 to 4), based on peak ground acceleration and velocity. The higher the zone, the greater the seismic forces the structure must withstand. The zone of your plot is one of the first inputs the engineering firm sets in its calculation report.

Who it applies to

The code covers new constructions, including single-family houses, with requirements proportionate to the importance of the building: an ordinary dwelling is not treated like a hospital or a school, which must remain usable after an earthquake.

What it actually requires of a villa

  • A regular shape as far as possible: compact plans, well-distributed masses, no abrupt offsets from one floor to the next.
  • Tied foundations: the footings are connected by tie beams to prevent differential movements.
  • Ring beams and vertical ties that confine the walls and connect floors and columns.
  • Ductile reinforcement: closely spaced links at the ends of columns and beams, sufficient anchorage and lap lengths, well-reinforced joints.
  • No "short columns" created by spandrels or partial walls, which concentrate forces.

A revision under way

After the Al Haouz earthquake, the authorities began updating the code: a draft decree amending RPS 2000 (2011 version) has been prepared, notably to review the seismic classification of municipalities, pending a full update study. Your engineering firm must apply the texts in force on the date your permit is filed.

The engineering firm's role

It is the BET that turns the code into drawings: it calculates the seismic forces, sizes columns, beams and shear walls, and draws the reinforcement details. On site, inspecting the reinforcement before each pour is the step that ensures these calculations actually end up in the concrete.

Frequently asked questions

Does the seismic code apply to a single-family house?

Yes: new constructions, including villas, must comply with the seismic code, with requirements suited to the importance of the building.

What is RPS 2011?

It is the 2011 revised version of Morocco's seismic building code RPS 2000: among other things, it introduces five-zone zoning and is the reference for structural calculations.

Is the seismic code going to change?

Yes: after the Al Haouz earthquake, a draft decree amending the RPS was prepared, notably to review the classification of municipalities. The BET applies the texts in force at the time of the permit.
