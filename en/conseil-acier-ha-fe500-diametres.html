HA FeE500 rebar: diameters, cross-sections and weight per metre

To read a reinforcement drawing, estimate a steel order or check an invoice, you need to know the cross-section and weight per metre of each bar diameter.

Published 16 November 2026 · 5 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

HA FeE500 steel is the most widely used high-bond reinforcing steel in reinforced concrete in Morocco: "HA" refers to the ribs that improve the bond with the concrete, and "500" to its yield strength of 500 MPa. An HA10 weighs 0.617 kg per metre, an HA12 0.888 kg/m and an HA14 1.21 kg/m.

The weight can be found with the formula: weight (kg/m) ≈ 0.00617 × d², with d in millimetres, the density of steel being 7,850 kg/m³. The uses shown are trends; the diameter of each bar is set by calculation.

How to read a reinforcement drawing

A note such as "4 HA12" means four 12 mm bars, i.e. 4 × 1.13 = 4.52 cm² of steel. "Stirrups HA8 e = 15" means 8 mm stirrups spaced at 15 cm. For a slab, "HA10 e = 20" means 10 mm bars every 20 cm, i.e. about 3.9 cm² per metre of width. These cross-sections are the ones calculated by the engineer: replacing a diameter with a smaller one greatly reduces strength.

Calculating a steel weight

  • Column 3 m high with 4 HA12: 4 × 3 m × 0.888 = 10.7 kg, plus laps and stirrups.
  • HA8 stirrups with a developed length of 1 m every 15 cm over 3 m: 20 stirrups × 0.395 = 7.9 kg.
  • Add a margin for offcuts and laps, often from a few percent to more than 10% depending on how well the bending is optimised.

Quality and storage

Insist on certified steel, with the manufacturer's and grade markings, and check on the delivery note that it is indeed FeE500 and the diameters ordered. On site, store the steel off the ground; light surface rust is not a problem, but steel that flakes or loses material must be rejected. Soil, formwork release oil and dried cement impair bond.

The role of the engineering firm

The engineering firm calculates the steel cross-sections, translates them into diameters and spacings on the drawings, and can provide bar schedules that allow precise ordering. It then checks on site, during the reinforcement inspection, that the diameters placed are indeed those on the drawings. For the associated concrete, see concrete classes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does one metre of 12 mm rebar weigh?

An HA12 bar weighs 0.888 kg per metre, i.e. about 10.7 kg for a 12 m bar. Its cross-section is 1.13 cm².

What does FeE500 mean?

It is a high-bond steel with a guaranteed yield strength of 500 MPa; it is the most common grade for reinforced concrete.

How do you calculate the weight of a steel bar?

Weight (kg/m) ≈ 0.00617 × d², with d in millimetres: for an HA10, 0.00617 × 100 = 0.617 kg/m.
