Building permit in Morocco: steps, Rokhas file and timelines

From the planning information note to the site notice board: the steps of the building permit, the documents in the Rokhas file and what costs you weeks.

Published 16 November 2026 · 7 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

In Morocco, no new construction can start without a building permit. Since planning procedures went digital, the application is filed online on the national Rokhas platform, but the substance of the work has not changed: a project that complies with the planning document, plans signed by an architect and consistent technical studies.

The steps, in order

  1. Planning information note: it states what the development plan allows on your plot (height, number of storeys, setbacks, plot ratio). It is the starting point for the sketch design.
  2. Design by the architect: hiring an architect is mandatory for the permit. The architect draws up the plans and puts together the application.
  3. Technical studies by the engineering firm (BET): structural calculations and reinforced concrete drawings, taking into account the seismic code and, depending on the project, the soil study.
  4. Online filing on Rokhas: documents are submitted in digital form, fees are paid and the application is tracked online. A dated acknowledgement of receipt is issued.
  5. Review: the municipality and the relevant departments (urban agency, civil protection depending on the project…) examine the file and may request additional documents.
  6. Issue and display: once the permit is granted, a site board showing its references must be displayed.
  7. Completion of works: the occupancy permit (or certificate of conformity) certifies that the completed building matches the permit.

The documents in the file

DocumentProvided by
Certificate of ownership (land title) and cadastral or topographic planOwner, surveyor
Architectural drawings (location, site plan, floor plans, sections, elevations) and architect's contractArchitect
Reinforced concrete studies and drawings, structural calculationsEngineering firm
Soil study, depending on the nature and size of the projectGeotechnical laboratory
Specific documents (fire safety for a building open to the public, etc.)Architect and BET

The exact list depends on the municipality and the type of project: check it on Rokhas before filing, because an incomplete application is not reviewed.

How long does it take?

Timelines vary widely from one municipality to another and with the complexity of the project. In practice, allow from a few weeks to three or four months between filing a complete application and the permit being issued, not counting the design and study time beforehand. Law 12-90 on urban planning provides that if no answer is given within two months of filing, the permit is deemed granted; in practice, a written permit is preferable. The permit also has a period of validity: if works do not start in time, it must be renewed.

Mistakes that block an application

  • A project that exceeds what the planning information note allows (height, setback, footprint).
  • Architect's drawings and reinforced concrete drawings that do not match (column grid, levels).
  • Illegible or badly named documents in the online filing.
  • No soil study when the municipality or the size of the project requires one.

The engineering firm gets involved early to avoid the second point: a structure designed from the sketch stage avoids back-and-forth with the municipality.

Frequently asked questions

Is a building permit mandatory for a villa in Morocco?

Yes: every new construction requires a building permit, which is now filed online on the Rokhas platform with the architect's plans and the technical studies.

How long does it take to get a building permit?

Allow from a few weeks to three or four months after filing a complete application, depending on the municipality and the complexity of the project.

Which technical studies are needed for the permit?

The engineering firm's reinforced concrete studies (calculations and drawings) and, depending on the project, a soil study and fire safety studies.
