What does a technical engineering firm do in your project?

Architect, BET, inspection body: who does what, when the engineering firm steps in, and why it often saves more than it costs.

Published 9 November 2026 · 6 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

On a Moroccan building site, people often talk about "the BET" without always knowing exactly what it does. Yet it is the BET that turns the architect's plans into a building that stands up, is supplied with water and electricity, and stays comfortable in summer and winter alike.

Architect, BET, inspection body: three distinct roles

PartyMain role
ArchitectDesigns the project: siting, volumes, plans, elevations. Files the building permit.
Technical engineering firm (BET)Sizes the structure and the building services from the architect's plans, produces the construction drawings and follows their implementation.
Technical inspection body (BCT)Independently checks that the drawings and the works comply with good practice and regulations.

As Médias24 sums it up, the BET works mainly at the preliminary design and construction stages: it relies on the soil study and the architect's sketches, coordinates with the developer, the contractors and the inspection body, then checks during the works that construction follows the studies.

What a BET actually does

  • Reinforced concrete: load calculation, foundations suited to the soil, columns, beams, slabs, reinforcement, taking into account the Moroccan seismic code.
  • Power systems (CFO): power balance, distribution boards, cable sizes, protective devices, lighting, earthing.
  • Low-current systems (CFA): IT network, telephone, television, intercom, alarm, CCTV.
  • Plumbing: cold and hot water supply, drainage, sizing of networks and equipment.
  • HVAC and ventilation: air conditioning, heating, air renewal, consistent with the thermal requirements.
  • Quantities and estimates: quantities and costs trade by trade, to tender to contractors on a clear basis.

The regulatory obligations the BET handles

Several texts govern construction in Morocco. The thermal building code (RTCM), approved by decree no. 2-13-874 of 15 October 2014, sets minimum thermal performance levels by climate zone; since 2015 it has applied to new residential and tertiary buildings, excluding individual rural housing. The guide published for its implementation states that the BET advises the architect on the choice of materials to achieve these performance levels. On the same day, decree no. 2-14-499 approved the fire and panic safety regulations for buildings, which are decisive for hotels, shops and apartment buildings.

When should you call in the BET?

As early as possible, ideally from the sketch design. A structural grid chosen at the start costs little; the same change during construction costs a lot. In practice, the BET's involvement unfolds in four stages:

  1. Preliminary design: taking the soil study into account, structural and building services principles, first estimate.
  2. Construction studies: calculation reports, formwork and reinforcement drawings, electrical and plumbing/HVAC diagrams.
  3. Contractor tendering: bills of quantities, specifications, comparison of bids.
  4. Site supervision: visits, inspection of reinforcement before pouring, answers to contractors' questions, handover.

A cost that pays for itself

A structure oversized "to be safe" uses unnecessary concrete and steel; an undersized structure exposes you to cracks and costly remedial work. A poorly designed electrical network means cutting new chases in finished walls. The BET avoids both pitfalls: it is often the fee item that produces the most direct savings on the structural shell.
