Building inspection cost in Morocco: fees and report content

Property valuation, technical assessment, court-appointed expert report: these services differ in both content and price. Here is how to tell them apart.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

In Morocco, a standard property valuation generally costs 3,500 to 8,000 DH excl. VAT according to a specialist firm, for a house or a flat. The price of a technical assessment by an engineering firm depends mainly on the number of trades inspected, the floor area and the investigations (probing, measurements). These amounts are indicative: always ask for a quote that specifies the content of the report.

Three services not to confuse

ServicePurposeWho carries it out
Property valuationEstimate the price of the property (sale, loan, inheritance)Property valuer
Pre-purchase technical assessmentIdentify defects and cost the workEngineering firm, engineer
Court-appointed expert reportEstablish the causes of a dispute at the request of a courtExpert registered with the courts

A property valuation does not always include a technical assessment or a search for hidden defects: check what is included before ordering.

What makes the price of a technical assessment vary

  • Floor area and number of storeys: a flat can be inspected in one to two hours, a villa or riad in half a day.
  • Trades covered: structure only, or structure, damp, electrics, plumbing and drainage.
  • Investigations: moisture measurement, testing the electrical earth, rebar detection, concrete coring or foundation probing are charged extra.
  • Level of report: a simple opinion, a photo report by trade, or a report with a costed estimate of the work.
  • Travel: a property located outside the city or in the medina, with difficult access.

Indicative price benchmarks

  • Standard property valuation: 3,500 to 8,000 DH excl. VAT, according to a valuation firm; report in about a week.
  • Soil survey for a villa: generally 5,000 to 10,000 DH. See our article on soil surveys.
  • Full technical studies for a project: see our article on the cost of an engineering firm.

Compared with the price of a house, the cost of an assessment remains low: a single defect detected (waterproofing, electrical installation, structural crack) can represent a much larger sum, which you can negotiate before signing.

Choosing the right expert

  • Ask about qualifications: a structural engineer for cracks, a building services engineer for the networks.
  • Insist on a written quote listing the trades, the measurements and the report format.
  • Check independence: the expert must not be connected to the seller or to the company that will carry out the work.
  • Have the inspection done before the preliminary agreement, so that you can negotiate or walk away.
  • Attend the visit: the expert shows you the defects on site, which makes the report easier to understand and to use with the seller.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a property inspection cost in Morocco?

According to a specialist firm, a standard valuation costs between 3,500 and 8,000 DH excl. VAT. A technical assessment by an engineering firm is priced according to the floor area, the trades inspected and the measurements taken.

Who pays for the pre-purchase inspection?

Generally the buyer, since it serves to protect their decision and negotiate the price; nothing prevents a different arrangement being agreed with the seller.

Does a property valuation detect hidden defects?

Not necessarily: it is used to estimate the price. To identify technical defects, you need an assessment by an engineer or an engineering firm.
