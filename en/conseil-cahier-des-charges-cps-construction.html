Construction specifications (CPS): content and why you need them

Without specifications, each contractor prices a different building and you can neither compare bids nor demand the promised quality.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

The CPS (cahier des prescriptions spéciales, special specifications) is the document that describes precisely what the contractor must supply and how: materials, grades, standards, construction methods, tests and the administrative conditions of the contract. Together with the drawings, it forms the basis of the contract. Systematically used in Moroccan public contracts, it is just as useful for a villa or a riad.

What a CPS contains

PartContent
Administrative clausesScope, contract documents, deadline, penalties, retention money, insurance, acceptance, dispute resolution
Technical specifications per tradeStructural work, waterproofing, plumbing, electrical, air conditioning, joinery, finishes…
Bill of pricesList of work items with their unit (m³, m², linear m, unit) and the definition of each price
Cost estimatePlanned quantities, used to calculate the amount of each bid

In public contracts, the general clauses are set by the general administrative conditions applicable to works contracts (CCAG-T); the CPS supplements and details them for each project.

Examples of useful specifications

  • Concrete: strength class per element, mix proportions, curing, compression tests.
  • Steel: grade (FeE500), concrete cover, inspection before pouring.
  • Electrical: cable cross-sections, brands and ranges of fittings, 30 mA residual current devices, technical reference (NF C 15-100 or Moroccan standards NM 06.1.100 and following).
  • Plumbing: type of pipes (PPR, PEX, multilayer), slopes, pressure tests.
  • Insulation: type and thickness of roof insulation, consistent with the RTCM thermal regulation.

Why it protects you

  1. Comparing bids: all contractors price the same quantities and the same grades; the price difference becomes clear.
  2. Avoiding extras: what is described is included; omissions are rare.
  3. Checking the work: the BET and the client check against a written basis.
  4. Settling disputes: in the event of disagreement, the text prevails.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Copying a CPS from another project without adapting it.
  • Writing “in accordance with good practice” without specifying which practice.
  • Leaving contradictions between drawings and CPS without setting the order of precedence of documents.

How many pages? For a villa, a complete CPS often runs to a few dozen pages, including the bill of prices; for a hotel or an apartment building, much more. Length matters less than precision: each price in the bill must refer to a clear specification, and each specification must be consistent with the drawings. Also specify the required tests (concrete, network pressure, earth measurement) and the list of documents to be handed over at the end of the works.

The role of the engineering consultancy

Frequently asked questions

What does CPS mean in construction?

Cahier des prescriptions spéciales (special specifications): the document setting the administrative conditions and technical requirements specific to a project, with the bill of prices and the cost estimate.

Is a CPS useful for a private villa?

Yes: it allows bids to be compared on an identical basis, avoids extras and makes it possible to check the quality actually delivered.

Who writes the CPS?

The architect and the engineering consultancy, each for their own trades; the BET also prepares the bill of prices and the cost estimate.
