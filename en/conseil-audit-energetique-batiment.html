Energy audit of a building in Morocco: content and obligation

An energy audit shows where a building's energy goes and which actions pay off the most. For some companies, it is mandatory in Morocco.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

An energy audit is a detailed analysis of the consumption of a building or site, resulting in a costed action plan: expected savings, cost, payback period. In Morocco, it is mandatory for the highest-consuming establishments under Law No. 47-09 on energy efficiency and Decree No. 2-17-746 of 10 April 2019. For other buildings, it remains voluntary but is often very cost-effective.

Who is obliged?

SectorAnnual consumption threshold (according to the specialist press)
Industry, including energy production1,500 toe (tonnes of oil equivalent)
Tertiary sector, transport, energy distribution500 toe

The establishments concerned must have the audit carried out by an approved body and declare their consumption to the Moroccan Agency for Energy Efficiency (AMEE). The exact frequency and procedures should be checked in the text of the decree. A large hotel, a clinic or a shopping centre can reach the tertiary threshold.

The content of an audit

  1. Data collection: electricity, gas and fuel oil bills over at least one year, drawings, equipment, occupancy hours.
  2. Visit and measurements: building envelope (insulation, glazing), air conditioning, hot water production, lighting, pumps, kitchen, swimming pool; consumption logging if necessary.
  3. Assessment: breakdown of consumption by use, comparison with benchmark ratios.
  4. Action plan: measures ranked by cost-effectiveness, from free adjustments to major investments.

Typical actions identified

  • No investment: temperature set points, operating hours, switching off unused equipment.
  • Low investment: LEDs, variable speed drives on pumps and fans, controls.
  • Medium investment: roof insulation, solar shading, heat recovery from chillers for hot water.
  • Major investment: replacement of the cooling plant, collective solar water heating, photovoltaics.

What about a villa or a riad?

A private house is not subject to the obligation, but a simplified diagnosis helps target spending: should you insulate the terrace, change the air conditioners or install solar? See reducing a villa's electricity bill. For a guest house, energy has a direct impact on profitability.

How much does an audit pay back? Savings depend on the starting building. In an establishment that has never been optimised, the first adjustment and management measures often bring quick gains without investment; works then follow according to their payback period. The value of the audit lies precisely in costing each action so as to avoid investing first in what pays back the least. It also serves as a baseline for monitoring consumption after the works and checking that the announced savings are actually achieved.

Frequently asked questions

Is an energy audit mandatory in Morocco?

Yes for establishments exceeding certain consumption thresholds, under Law 47-09 and Decree 2-17-746: 1,500 toe per year in industry and 500 toe in the tertiary sector according to the specialist press.

Who can carry out a mandatory energy audit?

An energy audit body approved by the government authority in charge of energy.

Is an audit useful for a villa?

A villa is not subject to the obligation, but a simplified diagnosis helps choose between insulation, replacing air conditioners and solar according to their cost-effectiveness.
