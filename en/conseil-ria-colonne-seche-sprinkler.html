Fire hose reel, dry riser, sprinkler: differences and use cases

Three water-based systems that are often confused: one for staff, another for firefighters, the third that acts on its own.

Published 16 November 2026 · 5 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

The RIA (fire hose reel) is a semi-rigid hose kept permanently under pressure, usable by staff in the first minutes. The dry riser is an empty pipe that firefighters connect to their appliance to get water on the upper floors. The sprinkler system is a network of ceiling heads that open automatically under the effect of heat. Whether they are required depends on the building's height, use and risks: it is the fire safety regulation (Decree 2-14-499) and the project study that impose them.

The fire hose reel

  • Common diameters DN 25 or DN 33, with a hose of 20 or 30 m (European standard EN 671-1).
  • Minimum operating pressure of around 3 to 3.5 bar according to manufacturers' data sheets: a dedicated booster is often required.
  • Layout such that every point of the space is reached by at least one jet, near accesses and staircases.

The dry riser

It comprises an inlet connection on the facade, accessible to fire appliances, an empty vertical pipe and outlets on each floor, generally in the protected staircases. Being empty in normal times, it does not freeze or leak, but it must be pressure-tested periodically. The height thresholds above which it is required differ between regulations: use those of the Moroccan text for your project.

The sprinkler

Each head is closed by a glass bulb filled with liquid; under the effect of heat, the liquid expands and the bulb bursts. The colour indicates the activation temperature: orange 57 °C, red 68 °C (the most common), yellow 79 °C, green 93 °C. Only the heads close to the fire open: the rest of the building is not flooded, contrary to a widespread belief. The design follows references such as standard EN 12845 or the APSAD R1 rule.

Water is the key. In Marrakech as elsewhere, the pressure and flow of the public network are not always sufficient, and supply cuts do occur. A fire water reserve and a pumping set, with a backed-up power supply, are often necessary. The volume and flow are calculated in the study.

The role of the engineering firm

The engineering firm determines the required extinguishing systems, sizes the networks, the reserve and the pumps, coordinates with plumbing and structure (weight of tanks, penetrations) and writes the specifications. See also fire extinguishers and water tanks.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a fire hose reel and a dry riser?

The fire hose reel is a pressurised hose usable by staff; the dry riser is an empty pipe that firefighters supply with their appliance to get water on the upper floors.

At what temperature does a sprinkler activate?

The most common head, with a red bulb, activates at 68 °C; there are also heads at 57, 79 or 93 °C depending on the room's ambient conditions.

Does a sprinkler flood the whole building?

No: only the heads heated by the fire open, the others remain closed.
