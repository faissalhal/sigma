Reducing a villa's electricity bill in Morocco: the main levers

In a villa in Marrakech, air conditioning, hot water and the swimming pool account for most of the bill. Here are the levers, ranked by effectiveness.

Published 16 November 2026 · 3 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

To reduce a villa's electricity bill in Morocco, act first on the three biggest items: air conditioning, hot water production and the swimming pool pump. The residential tariff is progressive by consumption bands: every kilowatt-hour saved at the top of the bill is the most expensive one. A tariff reform is also being prepared by the regulator (ANRE), with entry into force announced for 2027: reducing your consumption is lasting protection.

Air conditioning: the biggest item

  1. Every degree counts: setting 25-26 °C instead of 21-22 °C significantly reduces consumption, with genuine comfort if the air is dry.
  2. Only cool occupied rooms: a zoned system is better than all or nothing.
  3. Clean the filters and have the units serviced: a clogged heat exchanger uses more energy for the same cooling.
  4. Shade the outdoor unit without obstructing air flow.
  5. Reduce the need: insulation and solar shading often make it possible to reduce the installed capacity. See air conditioning capacity.

Hot water and solar

With the sunshine in Marrakech, a solar water heater covers most needs for much of the year. For electricity itself, photovoltaic panels for self-consumption reduce daytime consumption, precisely when the air conditioning runs the most; their legal framework is set by Law 82-21 on self-generation. See photovoltaics and Law 82-21.

Start by measuring

Read your meter every week for a month, noting your use of the air conditioning and the pool. Sub-metering per circuit at the panel makes it possible to identify precisely which item is drifting.

Common mistakes. Leaving the air conditioning running with doors and windows open, installing an oversized air conditioner that cycles on and off, heating water with an electric storage heater set too high, or running the pool pump all day in winter: these habits weigh heavily on a bill. Also check for hidden consumption, such as a booster pump that starts constantly because of a small leak.

Frequently asked questions

What is the largest consumption item in a villa in Marrakech?

Most often air conditioning in summer, followed by hot water production and, if there is one, the swimming pool pump.

What temperature should the air conditioning be set to?

Around 25 to 26 °C in summer: each degree lower significantly increases consumption for a limited gain in comfort.

Do solar panels really reduce the bill?

Yes for daytime consumption, which coincides with air conditioning; self-generation is governed by Law 82-21.
