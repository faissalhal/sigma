Subdivision VRD in Morocco: the steps from design to acceptance

Roads, sewerage, water, electricity, lighting, telecoms: the logical order of studies and works for a subdivision, and what determines when plots can be sold.

Published 16 November 2026 · 6 min read · SIGMA ICC, engineering consultancy

The VRD (roads and utility networks) of a subdivision unfold in four stages: the subdivision permit, the technical studies approved by the network operators, the works carried out in a precise order (from deepest to shallowest) and acceptance by the municipality. Until this provisional acceptance has been granted, the plots can in principle be neither sold nor built on under normal conditions.

1. The subdivision permit

In Morocco, creating a subdivision falls under Law 25-90 on subdivisions, housing groups and land divisions. It requires prior authorisation issued by the president of the municipal council, and it may be refused if the land cannot be connected to the road, sewerage, drinking water and electricity networks. In addition to the urban planning drawings, the application therefore includes the technical VRD documents. Law 34-21, which amends Law 25-90, was published in the Official Bulletin in August 2026 according to the specialist press: among other things, it revises the time limits and the acceptance procedures. Have your architect and your engineering firm check which provisions apply to your project, as some implementing texts are still awaited.

2. The VRD studies

  • Topographic survey and, ideally, a geotechnical study: they determine the gradients, the earthworks and the pavement structure.
  • Roads: alignment, longitudinal and cross profiles, pavement structure, pavements, parking.
  • Sewerage: wastewater and stormwater, connection to the existing public network.
  • Drinking water: distribution network, fire hydrants or fire posts as required.
  • Electricity: medium-voltage network, transformer substations, low-voltage network and street lighting.
  • Telecommunications: ducts and draw pits.

Each network is submitted to the relevant operator (in Marrakech, the regional multi-service company that took over the activities of Radeema; elsewhere, ONEE or the local operator). Its approval of the drawings is essential before starting the works.

3. The order of works

  1. General earthworks and topsoil stripping.
  2. Sewerage first: it is the deepest network and works by gravity, so its gradients dictate everything else.
  3. Drinking water, then electricity and telecom networks, respecting the clearances between networks.
  4. Pavement layers (capping, sub-base, base), kerbs and gutters.
  5. Wearing course, pavements, street lighting, signage and green spaces.

Reversing this order is expensive: a sewer trench opened after the road has been built destroys a brand-new surface.

4. Acceptance

StageBenchmark (Law 25-90, 1992 regime)
Time limit to complete the infrastructure3 years, failing which the permit lapses (time limit revised by the reform)
Provisional acceptanceWithin 45 days of the declaration of completion
Final acceptanceIn principle one year after provisional acceptance

Under the reform, provisional acceptance entails the transfer of the roads, networks and green spaces to the municipal public domain, and the developer must remedy the defects found during the following year. The land registry may also require the provisional acceptance report before certain transactions.

The role of the engineering firm

The VRD engineering firm designs the networks, prepares the drawings and quantity surveys, obtains the operators' approvals and supervises the site through to acceptance: leak tests, sewer inspection, compaction checks and as-built drawings. For the administrative side, see also our article on the building permit and Rokhas.

Frequently asked questions

Can plots be sold before the VRD works are finished?

In principle no: the sale of plots is tied to the municipality's provisional acceptance of the infrastructure works, and the land registry may require the acceptance report.

Which network is laid first in a subdivision?

Sewerage, because it is the deepest network and works by gravity; then come drinking water, electricity and telecoms, followed by the road pavement.

What is the time limit for provisional acceptance of a subdivision?

Under Law 25-90, provisional acceptance had to take place within 45 days of the declaration of completion; Law 34-21 has revised these time limits, so check them for your project.
