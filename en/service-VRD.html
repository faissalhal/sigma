Our firm specializes in feasibility studies for road and utility network projects in urban areas. We work for private clients as well as companies and local authorities.

Our team is made up of qualified, experienced engineers and technicians with in-depth expertise in roads and utility networks. We have full command of the tools and techniques needed to produce high-quality studies in compliance with current standards.

We offer a wide range of services tailored to the specific needs of each client. We can carry out feasibility studies for the construction, renovation or extension of roads, pipelines, water supply and sewerage networks, gas and electricity networks, and more. We can also carry out traffic and road safety studies for urban developments.

Client satisfaction matters greatly to us, which is why we listen closely to understand our clients' needs and propose the right solutions. We are available to answer all their questions and support them throughout their projects.

By choosing SIGMA ICC, you choose quality and reliability. Contact us for a free, personalized quote. We will be glad to support your roads and utilities engineering projects!
